Frequently Ask Questions
Q1. How do I register on StudentHub?
A. Click the Registration page and fill in the required details.
Q2. How do I log in to my account?
A. Enter your Email ID and Password on the Login page.
Q3. How can I update my profile?
A. Open the Profile page and click the Edit Profile button.
Q4. Where can I check upcoming events?
A. Go to the Events page from the navigation menu.
Q5. How can I contact the college?
A. Visit the Contact page for email, phone number and address.
